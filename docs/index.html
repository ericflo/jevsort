    What is jevsort?

    A small Python library that ranks anything with AI judges: summaries, ideas, papers, candidates, prompts.

    1. Ask small questions. "Which of these two is clearer?" beats "score this 1–10".
    2. Ask both ways, couple the answers. Averaging A-first and B-first cancels position bias; the 1994 PKPD rule (or Bradley–Terry) turns pairwise answers into one ranking with probabilities, even when the judge contradicts itself.
    3. Only ask what matters. An active schedule picks the informative pairs and stops when the ranking settles.
